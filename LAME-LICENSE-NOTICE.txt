TV Audio Recorder - MP3 support

This application integrates libmp3lame (LAME) version 3.100,
licensed under LGPL 2.0 or later as stated by LAME's authors.
LAME source and license are available at:
https://lame.sourceforge.io/
https://github.com/lameproject/lame

The native library is compiled during GitHub Actions from public LAME
source code, including the project's native bridge located under
native/mp3_bridge.c. You can modify and rebuild the native component.
No ads or subscriptions are involved.
